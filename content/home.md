---
headline: "Learning that holds up when not everyone plays fair"
---
Modern machine learning is trained on data gathered from millions of sources,
on hardware spread across many machines, with feedback from people whose
incentives differ. Some of those sources will be wrong, and some will be
adversarial. We study what can still be learned in that setting, and what
cannot.

The group is part of the [Centre de Mathématiques Appliquées](https://cmap.ip-paris.fr)
at École Polytechnique and is led by El Mahdi El Mhamdi. Our work combines
theory, such as impossibility results and guarantees for robust aggregation,
with experiments on real systems.
